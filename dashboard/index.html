About the player data & coverage

Reported season cash is not a verified calendar-year W-2. Imported contract details have not been individually reviewed. All 32 teams have schedule-based calendars. Most players have prefilled estimates; records without positive usable cash require a verified amount. Bonus classification, camp windows, residence and individual attendance remain assumptions.
